Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

GPU-Action ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by ENPLabs Group, is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, partners, and hardware providers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a GPU cluster reservation or list your GPU capacity, we collect:

  • Company or individual name
  • Work or academic email address
  • GPU hardware specifications and quantity
  • Workload description and compute requirements
  • Requested rental duration

We may also collect anonymized analytics data such as browser type, referring URLs, and page interactions through privacy-respecting tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide exclusively to:

  • Match your compute requirements with available GPU cluster capacity
  • Contact you with allocation confirmations and pricing proposals
  • Verify hardware providers and bind nodes to our brokerage network
  • Send transactional system notifications related to your reservation

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing unrelated to your GPU compute request.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing or commercial purposes. Your data is used solely for matching and executing GPU compute transactions.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored securely on AWS infrastructure with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Reservation data is retained for up to 24 months to support ongoing cluster agreements, after which it is permanently deleted.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request permanent deletion of your data at any time
  • Opt out of all communications by replying "unsubscribe" to any email

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@gpu-action.com.

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal functional cookies only. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Any analytics in use are anonymized and aggregated, and do not identify individual users.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact:
